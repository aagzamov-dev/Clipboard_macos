# Clipboard 1.0.3

- Click a history row once to paste into your previous app. Enable automatic paste in Settings → Permissions first. Command/Shift-click still selects multiple entries.
- Larger, always-visible Preview (eye) and Copy icons on the right, with 36 × 36-point click areas. Preview and Copy keep the popup open.
- Popup opening ignores the initiating click and stale dismissal callbacks. Pending paste is cancelled when you dismiss or reopen, and repeated clicks cannot queue duplicate paste operations.
- Refresh keeps existing rows visible while loading, preserves scroll position, and avoids full-history ID queries for first-page selections. Hidden previews no longer load item details.

Use **Check for Updates…** to install. This release remains **ad-hoc signed and not notarized**; macOS may require renewing Accessibility permission for the updated copy. Existing history and preferences are preserved. No additional privacy permissions are required.

Automated validation uses fixture data and private pasteboards. The running local app was not replaced or launched for this release. Real destination-app acceptance, the full OS/hardware matrix, and Instruments performance measurements remain unverified; see VERIFICATION.md. A sent Command-V is not proof that every app accepted its contents.
